Hi, I'm Madison Douglas, a graphic designer passionate about building engaging and effective visual experiences. With a background in illustration and photography, I bring a versatile, concept-driven approach to my work. I’ve developed a strong skill set across branding, digital design, and visual storytelling, using tools like Adobe Creative Suite, Figma, and Procreate to bring ideas to life. I’m especially interested in projects that blend creativity with strategy and create meaningful connections with audiences. I’m currently seeking opportunities to grow as a designer and collaborate on impactful work—feel free to explore my portfolio or reach out to connect.

About Madison

I’m someone who’s always been drawn to creativity in everyday life, especially through cartoons, character-driven stories, and anything a little expressive or offbeat. A lot of how I see the world is shaped by those influences—they’ve stuck with me and continue to inspire how I think and create.

I spend a lot of time sketching, watching animated shows, listening to a variety of music genres, watching police procedural dramas, or hanging out with my cat, Henry, who somehow always ends up involved in whatever I’m doing.

I’m also proudly queer, and being a lesbian is an important part of who I am—it shapes how I connect with others and how I move through the world. I also create illustration work under the name Athena Jade, which is something I came up with in middle school and never really let go of—it stuck, so I still sign my work as Athena. I value authenticity, humor, and individuality, and I’m always drawn to people and spaces that feel genuine and a little bit unique.
